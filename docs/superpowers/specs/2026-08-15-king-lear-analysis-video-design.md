# King Lear Analysis Video — Design

## Purpose

Create a 40-second, 16:9 DA Tuition educational video that demonstrates how a student can improve a shallow King Lear analysis into an embedded, layered, audience-linked paragraph.

## Audience and tone

Senior English students. The narration is a calm, clear female voice with an academically encouraging tone. The visual language is restrained and editorial: storm-black and navy foundations, high-contrast serif quote typography, clean sans-serif instructional text, and gold used only for the DA transformation transition.

## Story and timing

| Time | Beat | Screen treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:06 | Introduce the quotation and identify it as Act III of *King Lear*. | Crashing-wave background, quote revealed in short lines. |
| 0:06–0:12 | Present the supplied broad analysis. | A single analysis card over the storm. |
| 0:12–0:14 | Diagnose the analysis. | “Too broad”, “Too brief”, then “No context” pop in and out, individually, for 0.5 seconds each. |
| 0:14–0:17 | DA proposition. | “Struggling to Analyse? This is how DA can help!” |
| 0:17–0:22 | Teach quote embedding. | The supplied embedded-quote sentence holds for three seconds. |
| 0:22–0:29 | Teach layered technique analysis and thematic link. | The supplied deconstruction paragraph holds for three seconds; “pathetic fallacy”, “soliloquy”, and “the weather being a physical manifestation of his internal conflict” are bold. |
| 0:29–0:34 | Teach an impactful audience link. | The supplied audience-link sentence appears and is narrated. |
| 0:34–0:40 | Synthesis. | A gold streak crosses the storm and assembles the improved paragraph, holding for five seconds. |

## Exact on-screen copy

Opening quote:

> “Rage, blow, spit, fire; spout, rain! I tax not you, you elements with unkindness. I never gave you kingdom, called you children.”

Source: *King Lear*, Act III.

Initial analysis:

> Shakespeare uses pathetic fallacy in the quote “rage, blow, spit, fire; spout, rain!” to show his growing madness, making readers reflect on the instability of relationships.

Teaching cards and paragraphs use the user-supplied wording exactly. The final synthesis assembles the embedded quotation, layered analysis, and audience link as one coherent paragraph.

## Motion and sound

Use continuous leftward visual momentum across the teaching beats. The opening storm is active but never distracts from legibility. Diagnostic labels are the only sharp, rapid motion. The gold streak is the sole high-energy transition and carries the viewer into the final synthesis. Layer crashing-wave ambience, rainfall, distant thunder, and a quiet instructional music bed beneath the voice; duck all effects under narration.

## Deliverables and acceptance criteria

- 1920×1080 MP4, approximately 40 seconds, rendered with Remotion.
- Female narration covers the supplied teaching script.
- All requested text appears verbatim, with the requested emphasis in the layered-analysis paragraph.
- The final assembled paragraph is visible for at least five seconds.
- Ocean/storm imagery and rain/thunder sound are present, legible, and subordinate to the teaching content.
- A reviewable draft and source project are retained separately from the website application.
